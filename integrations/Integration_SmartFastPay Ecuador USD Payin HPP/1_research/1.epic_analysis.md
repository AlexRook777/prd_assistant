# Epic Analysis: IN-1137 SmartFastPay Ecuador USD Payin HPP

## 1. Summary of Requirements
*   **Goal**: Integrate SmartFastPay for **Ecuador (USD)** and **Peru (PEN)**.
*   **Method**: Initially **Bank Transfer**, followed by others.
*   **Flow**: **HPP (Hosted Payment Page)** via their checkout.
    *   User enters data on provider's page if not passed.
    *   If passed, fields are pre-filled.
*   **Traffic**: Offshore Gambling merchants.
*   **Limits**:
    *   **Ecuador**: Min $6, Max $1000. Daily $1200, Monthly $36000.
    *   **Peru**: Min 25 PEN, Max 3700 PEN. Daily 11100 PEN, Monthly 18500 PEN.
*   **Documentation**: [Methods and Markets - Ecuador Bank Transfer](https://docs.smartfastpay.com/methods-and-markets/payin/latam/equador/bank-transfer)
*   **Credentials**: Provided in `config` (`CLIENT_ID`, `CLIENT_SECRET`).

## 2. Unclear Points / Discrepancies

    *   *Assumption*: Proceeding with **HPP** as per the "Integration will be done via their checkout" statement.
