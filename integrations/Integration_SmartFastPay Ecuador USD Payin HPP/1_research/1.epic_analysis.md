**Date**: 2026-01-30

## Summary
- **Payment Method**: Bank Transfer
- **Countries**: Ecuador (Peru also mentioned in JIRA but focus is Ecuador)
- **Currencies**: USD (Ecuador), PEN (Peru)
- **Flow Type**: HPP (Redirect)
- **Transaction Types**: Payin

## BetterBro Alignment (Draft)
- **Service Rule**: payin_hpp
- **BetterBro Service URL**: [https://docs.betterbro.com/services/ecuador/bank_transfer/payin_hpp](https://docs.betterbro.com/services/ecuador/bank_transfer/payin_hpp)

## Requirements
- Standard HPP flow: Send request -> receive `pay_url` -> redirect user.
- Integration uses their [Checkout Experience](https://docs.smartfastpay.com/thesmartway/checkout-experience/overview).
- Mandatory/Recommended fields from merchants: `amount`, `name`, `tax_id_type`, `tax_id`, `email`.
- Limits for Ecuador:
  - Min: 6 USD
  - Max: 1,000 USD
  - Daily per user: 1,200 USD
  - Monthly per user: 36,000 USD
- Support for Spanish and English languages.

## Technical Constraints
- Documentation is noted as "not entirely up-to-date" in JIRA.
- Customer fields are optional in the create request (provider can collect them on HPP), but pre-filling is preferred.
- Test and Prod credentials available at `management@mailhub.services`.
