# Epic Analysis

**Integration Name**: SmartFastPay - Ecuador/Peru - USD/PEN - Payin HPP  
**Date**: 2026-01-28

## Summary
The objective is to integrate SmartFastPay for Payin HPP transactions. Initially, the focus will be on Bank Transfer for Ecuador and Peru, followed by other payment methods. The integration will use SmartFastPay's checkout experience (HPP), where a request is made to obtain a payment URL (payurl).

- **Payment Method**: Bank Transfer (primary), all payment methods eventually.
- **Countries**: Ecuador, Peru
- **Currencies**: USD (Ecuador), PEN (Peru)
- **Flow Type**: HPP (Hosted Payment Page)
- **Transaction Types**: Payin

## Requirements
- Support for Bank Transfer in Ecuador and Peru.
- Integration via Checkout Experience (HPP).
- Ability to pre-fill customer fields or allow the user to enter them on the provider's side.
- Conversion rate expectation: 70%+.
- Support for Spanish and English languages.

### Limits
#### Ecuador (USD)
- **Transaction**: Min 6, Max 1,000
- **Daily per user**: Max 1,200
- **Monthly per user**: Max 36,000

#### Peru (PEN)
- **Transaction**: Min 25, Max 3,700
- **Daily per user**: Max 11,100
- **Monthly per user**: Max 18,500
