# Epic Analysis

**Integration Name**: SmartFastPay - Ecuador / Peru - USD / PEN - Bank Transfer (HPP)  
**Date**: 2026-01-27

## Summary
- **Payment Method**: Bank Transfer (provider SmartFastPay)
- **Countries**: Ecuador (primary), Peru (mentioned)
- **Currencies**: Ecuador — USD, Peru — PEN
- **Flow Type**: HPP (Hosted Payment Page)
- **Transaction Types**: Payin

SmartFastPay HPP integration to enable bank-transfer payin for Ecuador (USD) with Peru (PEN) also specified in requirements. The Epic requests HPP flow (merchant will redirect users to provider page). Limits and per-user caps are provided in the source Jira export.

## Requirements
- Provide HPP pay-in integration for Ecuador (USD) and optionally Peru (PEN).
- Support provider-required fields: amount, payer name, tax_id_type, tax_id, email (per provider docs).
- Respect per-transaction and per-user limits:
  - Ecuador: min 6 USD, max 1,000 USD; per-user max daily 1,200 USD; monthly 36,000 USD
  - Peru: min 25 PEN (~7 USD), max 3,700 PEN (~1,000 USD); per-user max daily ~11,100 PEN (~3,000 USD); monthly ~18,500 PEN (~5,000 USD)
- Flow must be HPP (Hosted Payment Page). Note: HPP displays provider branding/name to merchants and customers.
- Language support: Spanish and English.
- Testing credentials and documentation links provided in source (SmartFastPay docs link included).
- Management / support contacts and some credentials available at management@mailhub.services (per ticket).

## Technical Constraints
- HPP will display provider name; this may expose provider to merchants and risk client loss.
- Expected daily/monthly volumes are not defined (TBD).
- Testing environment and Postman collection are referenced via provider docs link — confirm availability of sandbox credentials.
- Required fields (amount, name, tax_id_type, tax_id, email) must be collected by merchant / our request before redirecting to HPP.

## Unclear Points / Questions for Product Owner (PO)

4. Confirm whether Refunds and Payouts must be supported initially, or only Payin.  
5. Confirm SLA / response-time requirements and fraud/risk rules expected for this integration.  
6. Confirm who will be responsible for production support & payment operations (ticket shows `TBD`).  
7. Confirm availability of sandbox credentials and API endpoints for QA (ticket references docs and a spreadsheet).  

## BetterBro Compatibility
- HPP showing provider branding may conflict with merchant expectations and BetterBro's desire to keep provider details opaque. Suggested resolutions:
  - Prefer H2H or whitelabel solution if merchant retention is a concern.  
  - If HPP must be used, document the marketing / legal implications and get PO approval.

## Risks & Dependencies
- Dependency on SmartFastPay sandbox / test accounts and clear API / HPP documentation.  
- Branding exposure risk (HPP).  
- Unknown volumes may affect capacity planning and fraud thresholds.
