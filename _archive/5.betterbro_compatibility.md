## 4. Automation & Logic
- **Architecture Type**: HPP (Hosted Payment Page)
- **Deterministic**: Yes. Redirection and webhook notification follow a standard pattern.
- **Webhooks**: Standard POST JSON. Signature verification is required (HMAC logic to be verified during coding).

## 5. Blocking Issues & Risks
- [RISK] **Document Validation**: Ecuador has strict CI checksums. The BetterBro layer must ensure valid document formats are passed.
- [RISK] **Bank Transfer Latency**: Payments are not always real-time; our system must be robust enough to handle the `pending` state for prolonged periods.

- [x] **GREEN LIGHT**: Fully compatible, proceed to implementation.
