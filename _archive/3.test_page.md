## Usage Instructions
1. **Prerequisites**: Ensure the local `serve.py` is running (`python3 app/serve.py`).
2. **Open the Page**: Open [3.test_page.html](file:///home/worker/repos/prd_assistant/integrations/Integration_SmartFastPay%20Ecuador%20USD%20Payin%20HPP/1_result/3.test_page.html) in your browser.
3. **Configure Proxy**: Enable "Use Proxy" if you encounter CORS issues during local testing.
4. **Enter Credentials**: Fill in your Client ID and Secret (pre-filled if available in `integration_config`).
5. **Get Token**: Click "Get Token" to authenticate.
6. **Create Transaction**: Adjust amount and mandatory customer fields as needed, then click "Create Transaction".
7. **Test Payment**: Click the generated payment link to test the HPP flow.
8. **Verify Webhooks**: Use the tabs at the bottom to view API logs and incoming webhooks.

## Webhook Modes
- **Local**: Webhooks will be sent to `http://localhost:8000/webhook` and can be viewed in the "Webhooks" tab.
- **N8n**: Webhooks will be sent to n8n infrastructure for more robust testing.
